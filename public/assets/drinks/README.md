# Custom Drink Artwork Assets

By default, Cocktail Merge renders all 12 side-view cocktail glasses procedurally at high resolution.

If you would like to use your own custom PNG or SVG artwork:
1. Place your image files in this directory with the following naming convention:
   - `drink_0.png` or `drink_0.svg` (Citrus Splash)
   - `drink_1.png` or `drink_1.svg` (Berry Fizz)
   - `drink_2.png` or `drink_2.svg` (Pineapple Cooler)
   - `drink_3.png` or `drink_3.svg` (Sunset Cooler)
   - `drink_4.png` or `drink_4.svg` (Mint Lime)
   - `drink_5.png` or `drink_5.svg` (Tropical Punch)
   - `drink_6.png` or `drink_6.svg` (Island Breeze)
   - `drink_7.png` or `drink_7.svg` (Blue Lagoon)
   - `drink_8.png` or `drink_8.svg` (Passion Colada)
   - `drink_9.png` or `drink_9.svg` (Golden Sunset)
   - `drink_10.png` or `drink_10.svg` (Royal Cocktail)
   - `drink_11.png` or `drink_11.svg` (Ultimate Cocktail)
2. Square dimensions (e.g. 256x256 or 512x512) with transparent backgrounds work best.
